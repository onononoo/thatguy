thatguy / faq

what is this?
a website that shows you a random crypto address every time you look at it. that's it. that's thatguy.
are the addresses real?
they are correctly formatted, with valid checksums, for a lot of different coins. paste one into a wallet and it will say it's a real address.
can i send coins to one?
no. the addresses come from random bytes, not from a private key. nobody has the key, not you, not me, not anyone. anything sent there is gone forever.
so what's the point?
there isn't one. use them as placeholders for testing, for mockups, for screenshots, or just to stare at.
how does it work?
your browser picks random bytes and wraps them in the right format for the coin: base58check for bitcoin-style coins, bech32 or bech32m for segwit and cosmos-style chains, eip-55 for ethereum-style chains, cashaddr, ss58, stellar, nano, ton and a few more. the hashes (sha-256, keccak-256, blake2b) are plain javascript in js/hash.js.
does it send anything anywhere?
no. no server, no trackers, no analytics. it all happens on your computer. your history, your count and the fake price live in your browser's local storage.
what is TGUY?
not a coin. the price is a random walk that only exists in your browser. please do not ask where to buy it.
what does "luck" mean?
it's the first byte of the address's sha-256, mod 101. it means nothing. it's accurate, though.
how long would it take to crack one?
the "time to crack" stat assumes a trillion guesses per second. the sun will be gone long before that finishes.
keyboard shortcuts?
on the main page: space or n for another one, c to copy, v to change the view.
is this financial advice?
it is the opposite of financial advice.
can i run it myself?
yes. download the source and open index.html. no build step, no installs.
who is thatguy?
you are now.
